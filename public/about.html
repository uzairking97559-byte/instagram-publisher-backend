About this service

Creator Publishing Tool ek independent third-party service hai. Iska Meta Platforms, Instagram, ya Facebook se affiliation, endorsement, ya sponsorship nahi hai.

Account sign-in

Instagram account connect karne par browser Instagram ke official sign-in page par jaata hai. Facebook option Facebook ke official sign-in page par khulta hai. Is app mein apna Instagram ya Facebook password mat daaliye. Agar aap dashboard password use karte hain, woh sirf is app ke dashboard ke liye hai.

Account access

Aapki permission ke baad yeh app Meta ke published APIs se eligible professional Instagram accounts connect karta hai. App ko authorization token milta hai, jise account connection aur aapke request ki publishing ke liye use kiya jaata hai. Yeh app aapse Instagram/Facebook password nahi maangta.

Disconnect

Dashboard mein Disconnect karne se is app mein saved account connection hat jaata hai, aur us account ki queue mein baaki Reels cancel ho jaati hain. Meta ko di hui permission alag se band karni ho to apne Meta account settings mein app access revoke karein.
